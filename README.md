# Ground Shadow Contrast

Tired of having to fiddle with every setting known to mankind in your game's environment just so you can avoid getting hit? Not feeling like turning off 117 HD and turning your floor into a single colored boring mess?
A visual accessibility plugin for Old School RuneLite that standardizes faint ground shadow decals and dangerous falling projectiles into high-contrast, circular ground indicators.

## Features
- **High-Contrast Indicators:** Standardizes subtle floor decals and falling hazards into clear, configurable circular markers with high-visibility borders.
- **Configurable Aesthetics:** Adjust indicator radius, core fill color/opacity, and boundary ring color to suit your contrast preferences.
- **Per-Boss Toggles:** Fully toggleable checkboxes for every supported encounter.
- **Dynamic Timing Telegraph:** Tombs of Amascut Warden P4 lightning indicators expand from 50% to 100% radius across their 3-tick telegraph window, meeting the boundary ring at the exact moment of impact.

## Supported Encounters
### Raids
- **Chambers of Xeric (Olm):** Falling stalactites
- **Tombs of Amascut (Warden P4):** Expanding lightning warning decals
- **Tombs of Amascut (Ba-Ba):** Falling ceiling rubble & shockwave slams
- **Theatre of Blood (Bloat):** Falling flesh & limbs

### Bosses & Monsters
- **Tormented Demons:** Firebomb special projectile targets
- **The Maggot King:** Falling carrion shadows
- **Phosani's Nightmare:** Grasping claws floor decals
- **Doom of Mokhaiotl:** Exploding boulder landing tiles
- **The Leviathan:** Enrage phase falling ceiling debris
- **Grotesque Guardians:** Falling rocks & lightning zones
- **Demonic Gorillas:** Falling ceiling boulder attacks
